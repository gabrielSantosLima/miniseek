# Bibliography and Local References

All files in `docs/references/` were downloaded on **2026-11-08** from the URLs
listed below. They are kept locally so the study guide can be read offline.

> **License note:** the PDFs are the copyright of their respective authors and
> publishers. They are stored here as personal study copies. Before redistributing
> or publishing this repository, check each license and replace or remove any file
> that cannot be redistributed. Open-access papers are marked **OA**; the two
> books from MIT Press / Cambridge are freely available online but still carry
> their own license terms.

## 2. Required texts not stored locally

These are copyrighted books. Use a legally obtained copy (library, purchase, or
institutional access). They are central to the plan and should not be skipped.

| Text | Why it is needed | Official / legal access |
|---|---|---|
| Stewart, J. *Calculus: Early Transcendentals*, 8th or 9th ed., Cengage. | Primary calculus reference for Chapters 2–5. | https://www.cengage.com/c/calculus-early-transcendentals-8e-stewart/ |
| Stewart, J. *Calculus*, 9th ed., Cengage. | Alternative edition; section numbering is close. | https://www.cengage.com/c/calculus-9e-stewart/ |

> **Local study copies (not in this repository):** the learner keeps
> *Cálculo, Volume 1* and *Cálculo, Volume 2* (Stewart, 7th Brazilian edition)
> in the local `Downloads` folder. They are intentionally excluded from Git.
> Volume 1 covers Chapters 1–8 (functions, limits, derivatives, applications,
> integrals); Volume 2 covers Chapters 9–17 (sequences and series, vectors,
> partial derivatives, multiple integrals). The guide cites the corresponding
> sections in its reading lists.
| Strang, G. *Introduction to Linear Algebra*, 6th ed., Wellesley–Cambridge. | Companion for Chapter 6. | https://math.mit.edu/~gs/linearalgebra/ |
| Strang, G. MIT 18.06 *Linear Algebra* lectures. | Free video lectures and exercises for Chapter 6. | https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/ |
| Nielsen, M. *Neural Networks and Deep Learning*. | Alternative, very clear treatment of backpropagation and cross-entropy. | http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/ |
| Olah, C. *Understanding LSTM Networks*. | Best visual intuition for LSTM gates. | https://colah.github.io/posts/2015-08-Understanding-LSTMs/ |
| Rush, A., et al. *The Annotated Transformer*. | Line-by-line explanation of the Transformer. | https://nlp.seas.harvard.edu/annotated-transformer/ |
| Karpathy, A. *The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks*. | Intuition for character-level sequence models. | https://karpathy.github.io/2015/05/21/rnn-effectiveness/ |
| Karpathy, A. *Yes You Should Understand Backprop*. | Intuition for why backprop is subtle. | https://karpathy.medium.com/yes-you-should-understand-backprop-e2f06eab496b |
| Radford, A., et al. (2019). *Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners* (GPT-2). | Historical bridge from Transformer to LLM. | https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf |
| Chung, J., et al. (2014). *Empirical Evaluation of Gated Recurrent Neural Networks on Sequence Modeling.* | GRU vs LSTM empirical comparison. | https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.3555 |
